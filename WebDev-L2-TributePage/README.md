**# Steve Jobs Tribute Page**

**## About the Project**

The Steve Jobs Tribute Page is a responsive website dedicated to Steve Jobs, the co-founder of Apple Inc.

The website presents his biography, important career milestones, notable quote and lasting contributions to technology through a clean and minimalist design.

**## Features**

1. Hero section introducing Steve Jobs.
2. Biography covering his early life, career and achievements.
3. Timeline highlighting important events in his life.
4. Quote section featuring his famous Stanford commencement speech quote.
5. Legacy section highlighting his influence on technology.
6. Responsive layout for desktop and mobile devices.
7. Image attribution and licensing information.

**## Technologies Used**

1. HTML5
2. CSS3
3. Google Fonts
